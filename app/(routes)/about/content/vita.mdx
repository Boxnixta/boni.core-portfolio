Bonita von Gizycki is a designer and creative technologist based in Potsdam. She studies Creative Technologies (M.A.) at the Film University Babelsberg Konrad Wolf and holds a B.A. in Integrated Design from Anhalt University of Applied Sciences. Her work ranges from branding, illustration and editorial design to film, 3D animation, VR and creative coding.
