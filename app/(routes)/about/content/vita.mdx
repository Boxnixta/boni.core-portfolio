## TEXTUAL CV

*For programs, announcements, and similar contexts:*

**Boni.Core** is a **designer** and **creative technologist** working across immersive media, creative coding and digital storytelling. Her practice explores how technology, aesthetics and interaction shape perception, emotion and participation. Combining a background in integrated design with ongoing studies in Creative Technologies, she develops interactive experiences ranging from XR environments and experimental interfaces to audiovisual and generative systems.

I am particularly interested in the emotional dimensions of technology and how aesthetics shape our relationship with digital systems.
My work explores questions such as:
- *Why do some technologies feel approachable while others feel alienating?*
- *How do digital aesthetics influence perception, trust and participation?*
- *Can virtual environments create empathy through embodied experiences?*
- *How do nostalgia and cuteness shape contemporary technological culture?*
- *What happens when design, code and storytelling merge into a single medium?*
