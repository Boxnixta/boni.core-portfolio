## Context

Street harassment is often discussed but rarely felt by those who do not experience it. Grenzen uses the body-based nature of VR to shift perspective: being smaller, being approached, not being able to step away.

## Process

- Unity 6 LTS with the XR Interaction Toolkit and continuous locomotion
- Cesium for Unity for real 3D geospatial data of the neighborhood
- NPCs built in Character Creator 4, voiced with ElevenLabs
- Custom C# systems for NPC sequencing and audio management
- Perlin-noise-based mouth animation

## Results

A working MVP, completed in March 2026 after more than 100 hours of development. The biggest learning: the best solutions often came out of failures. Persistent debugging turned a steep learning curve into a stable architecture that goes beyond the initial plan.
