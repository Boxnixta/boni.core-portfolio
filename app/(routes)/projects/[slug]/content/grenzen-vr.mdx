## How it works

- **Real place:** Kottbusser Tor built from 3D geospatial data with Cesium for Unity
- **Smaller body:** a scaled XR rig makes you feel physically smaller and more exposed
- **NPCs:** characters from Character Creator 4 with AI voices (ElevenLabs), spatial audio and Perlin noise mouth movement
- **Story logic:** a character sequencer instead of physical triggers, so the scenes always play in the right order
