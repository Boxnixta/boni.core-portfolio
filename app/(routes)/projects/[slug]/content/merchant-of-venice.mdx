## Concept

Two color symbols carry the design: red for violence and tension, blue for the sea as a silent companion. Abstract shapes inspired by entrails reflect the emotional and brutal conflicts of the play, while thought and speech bubbles visualize the inner struggles of the characters. The result is an expressive, symbolically charged design that translates the depth of the drama visually.

This project was created as a fictional study project.
