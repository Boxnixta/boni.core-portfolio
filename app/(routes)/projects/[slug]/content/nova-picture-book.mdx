## Concept

Nova stands for strength in being different and becomes a tangible role model for young people through a child-friendly story. The concept includes a human-beaver fusion as the mascot, gender-neutral, emotional texts and a clear message: integration and community make us stronger. The book is meant to be used in schools and at the club to reach children and their families.
