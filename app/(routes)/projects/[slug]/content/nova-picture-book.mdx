My goal was to strengthen the club and its Biber-Akademie in supporting FLINTA people and families with a migration background. Nova stands for strength in being different and, through a child-friendly story, becomes a tangible role model for young people.

The concept includes a human-beaver fusion as the mascot, gender-neutral, emotional texts and a clear message: strengthen integration and community. The book is meant to be used in schools and in the club to reach children and their families and to share the values of the club.
