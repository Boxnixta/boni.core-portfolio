## Build

A Raspberry Pi 4 with a 5 inch Waveshare touchscreen, a mini wireless keyboard and flat ribbon cables, the game changer that made everything fit into the tiny bag. A second HDMI output is ready for a beamer. The mounts are my own designs, modelled in Blender, 3D printed and finished with modeling gel and chrome nail polish.

## Software

Raspberry Pi OS with VS Code, Git, Obsidian, Pure Data and Chromium, plus offline tools that fit the cyberdeck idea: offline Wikipedia (Kiwix), Organic Maps, SuperTuxKart and the retro RPG Moonring.

## Background & Next Steps

The build is linked to my short paper *Cuteness as a Mechanism of Access*, which asks how a cute aesthetic can lower the barriers to hardware and maker culture. Next up: a Pi Zero 2 W with battery for a fully wireless deck, better cooling and audio-reactive party visuals. The biggest win: I lost my fear of touching hardware.
