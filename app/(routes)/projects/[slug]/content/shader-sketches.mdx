## Spray Noise (GLSL, live)

A grainy red spray, like paint from a can. Your mouse works like a hand that pushes the spray away. Hash noise decides for every pixel whether a spray dot appears, the density fades from the center outwards, and a dot product makes the gap around the mouse asymmetric.

<ShaderCanvas frag={spray} ratio="16 / 9" label="move your mouse over the spray" />

Inspired by the photo "Sans titre" by Sébastien Fraysse (2015): the hand appears as a negative shape, exactly where no spray arrived.
