## The Article

Based on a personal interview with a woman who fled Ukraine, the article shows how fragmented stories of refugees appear in the media. Long exposures stand for time and identity blurring, typo art for the rush, the loss and the long waiting in lines. Typography, illustration and photography make fragments of memory, loss and changing perspectives tangible.

## The Magazine
