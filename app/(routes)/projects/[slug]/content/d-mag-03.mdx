## The Article

Based on a personal interview with a woman who fled Ukraine, the article shows how fragmented stories of refugees appear in the media. Typography, illustration and long exposures make fragments of memory, loss and changing perspectives tangible.

## The Magazine

As a team we shaped the dramaturgy of the magazine, edited the contents and prepared production, from paper choice to final print.
