## The Article (2022)

Long exposures symbolize the blurring of time and identity, while typo art expresses the rush, the loss and the long waiting in lines during the flight from war. Graphic elements and an immersive layout invite readers to change perspective, develop empathy and understand the situation of refugees.

## The Magazine (2023)

I was also part of the design and editorial development of D-Mag 03. My tasks included the cover design, writing and laying out the prologue, editing selected articles and co-designing the layout. As a team, we defined the dramaturgy of the magazine, refined the corporate look and prepared production decisions, from paper and typeface choice to the printing process.
