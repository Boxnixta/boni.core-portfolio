<h2 className="not-prose project-section-title">3001 Nacht</h2>
<p className="not-prose project-section-meta">Event reel · 3D animation · Motion design · 2025</p>

An animated Instagram reel for the second anniversary of the Berlin collective 3001 Nacht, made with Lea Georgiew (@lea_g.staltet) to go with her poster. From her sketches I built 3D shapes in Blender and the main acts with Human Generator, animated them with Mixamo and my own facial expressions, and graded the result in DaVinci Resolve with halation for a retro sci-fi glow. Made in three weeks.
