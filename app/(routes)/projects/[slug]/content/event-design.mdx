## Jazz Against The Machine Festival 2024

For the festival at Artheater Köln, I designed the official poster and curated the entire Instagram feed: 18 posts that work on their own and form a coherent picture in the grid. The festival combined musical genre and gender diversity with support for local newcomers, and the design shaped the visual identity of the event.

## Thalassas Oase 2025

For this wellness and party weekend, I created several 3D animations used as teasers in different formats. A steaming hot spring and an enchanted garden stand for calm, warmth and relaxed connection, and give a first taste of the weekend's world.

## Morph mal wieder & Morph nochmal 2022

Social media designs for club nights at Menschmeier and with Killi & Void.
