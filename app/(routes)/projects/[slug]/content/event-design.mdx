<h2 className="not-prose project-section-title">3001 Nacht</h2>
<p className="not-prose project-section-meta">Event reel · 3D animation · Motion design · Collaboration · 2025</p>

For the second anniversary of the Berlin collective 3001 Nacht, I created an animated Instagram reel together with Lea Georgiew (@lea_g.staltet) to go with her event poster. The goal was to carry the special vibe of the party, playful, futuristic and full of energy, and to translate the look of the poster into motion.

Based on Lea's sketches I built 3D shapes in Blender and the main acts with Human Generator. The figures were animated with Mixamo and brought to life with my own facial expressions. Event typography and graphic elements were built in 3D, animated and combined with a dynamic camera move. The colour grading in DaVinci Resolve uses halation for a retro sci-fi look with glowing neon colours. Music by Plattenlieferant, with sound tags by Plattenlieferant, S.3000 and the 3001 Nacht collective. The narrator's voice was created with AI.

The result: a consistent promo reel that captures the energy of the event and carries the poster into motion, made within three weeks.

<h2 className="not-prose project-section-title">Thalassas Oase</h2>
<p className="not-prose project-section-meta">Wellness & party event · 3D visuals for an immersive festival experience · 2025</p>

For Thalassas Oase (May 2025) I created several 3D animations that were used as visual teasers in different formats. The goal was to give an atmospheric foretaste of the weekend: relaxation, nature and a feeling of community. The animations show a steaming hot spring and an enchanted garden, symbols of calm, warmth and relaxed connection.

The design carries the concept of the event into the digital world and invites people to dive into Thalassas Oase before it even starts.

<h2 className="not-prose project-section-title">The Merchant of Venice</h2>
<p className="not-prose project-section-meta">"Dramaqueen" · Visual identity, poster and print design for a theatre play · 2021</p>

In a short project I developed a visual identity for The Merchant of Venice, focusing on the conflicts, emotions and central themes of the play. I designed posters, tickets, programmes, billboards and facade banners. Two colour symbols carry it: red for violence and tension, blue for the sea as a silent companion.

Abstract shapes inspired by entrails reflect the emotional and brutal conflicts of the play, while thought and speech bubbles show the inner struggles of the characters. The result is an expressive, symbolically charged design that translates the depth of the drama. The project was a fictional study project.
