## Concept

The piece follows the Gauntlet of Gods card game format: Eora is designed as a playable champion card with the ability "Afflict", a damage-over-time effect that fits her digital disintegration. Format: 1080×1920, 24 fps, 120 frames.

## Process

- **Niagara simulations:** Extended the course systems with curl noise, drag and deterministic physics for a punchy procedural burst
- **Mesh-based spawning:** Vertex color masks on the skeletal mesh so particles leave only defined body areas, synchronized to frame 63
- **Shading:** Custom glass and dissolve shaders that react to the simulation
- **Rendering:** Movie Render Queue as a 4K PNG sequence, finished in DaVinci Resolve. Sound design in InShot.

## Challenges

The render first looked different from the viewport: particles moved slower and glass refractions disappeared. Removing Game Overrides, switching Niagara to deterministic mode and resetting warm-up frames made viewport and render match. A MetaHuman Assembly bug in UE 5.7 was solved by shortening the project path and generating missing textures manually.
