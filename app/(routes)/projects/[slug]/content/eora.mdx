## Process

- **Niagara:** the course particle systems pushed further with curl noise, drag and deterministic physics
- **Particles from the body:** vertex color masks on the MetaHuman, so particles only leave the face and skin, right at frame 63
- **Shading:** custom glass and dissolve materials that react to the simulation
- **Render:** Movie Render Queue as a 4K PNG sequence, finished in DaVinci Resolve, sound design in InShot

## Learnings

Set Niagara to deterministic early when you sync it with Sequencer, and test-render often: Movie Render Queue settings can completely change how simulations and glass look. And beta features have real costs, a MetaHuman bug in UE 5.7 ate a lot of time.
