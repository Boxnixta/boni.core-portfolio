## How it works

- **AudioAnalyzer** turns sound into smooth 0 to 1 loudness values per band. Instead of an FFT it uses simple low, high and band pass filters, plus three extra values: how deep the sub bass sits, how bright the highs are and how hard a bass hit is.
- **Blob** is one layer: 240 points around a centre, moved by Perlin noise and scaled by loudness. Special effects: a bass zigzag, bass and high shimmer and drifting mid bubbles. Waves ripple from the outer to the inner blobs.
- **ofApp** connects everything and draws hand-built sliders without a UI library.

The browser version is a port of the openFrameworks original with the Web Audio API and Canvas.
