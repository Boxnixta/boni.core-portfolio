## Architecture

The project is split into three classes, each with one clear job:

- **AudioAnalyzer** reads a file or the microphone and turns the sound into smooth 0–1 loudness values per band. Instead of FFT it uses simple low-, high- and band-pass filters that update every sample. Extra values describe how deep the sub-bass sits, how bright the highs are and how hard a bass hit is.
- **Blob** is one layer: 240 points around a center, shifted by Perlin noise and scaled by loudness. Special effects like a bass zigzag, bass and high shimmer and drifting mid bubbles are steered by the analyzer values. Waves ripple inward from outer to inner blobs.
- **ofApp** connects everything and draws hand-built sliders and switches without a UI library.

## Learning

Real-time audio is fragile: reopening the microphone stream silently stopped sending data after a few tries, with no error. Keeping the stream open permanently fixed it. And a visually complex result benefits a lot from small, single-purpose classes.

Tools: openFrameworks (C++) · ofxAudioFile
