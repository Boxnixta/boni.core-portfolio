## Research

The thesis looks at body dysmorphic disorder, the effects of social media, self-optimization in a neoliberal context, and the influence of patriarchal and colonial structures on body perception. Methodically, I used a mixed-methods approach with a qualitative focus, analyzing secondary sources from sociology, psychology and cultural studies.
