## The Film

Reduced, retro-futuristic imagery with holographic elements shows how social norms shape the way we see ourselves. The mentor is a minimalist, genderless avatar: a reflective counterpart, never a replacement for human closeness.

## Making of

Research, script, storyboard and animatic came first. For the three shooting days I briefed a crew of younger students, so I could focus on directing and on the lead actress, who had never acted before. The mentor, the city and the rooms were built in Blender, compositing was done in After Effects.
