## Process

Based on Lea Georgiew's 2D Graphic Design, I built 3D shapes in Blender and created the main acts with Human Generator. The figures were animated with Mixamo and refined with my own facial expressions. Event typography and graphic elements were built in 3D and combined with a dynamic camera move. Color grading in DaVinci Resolve used halation for a retro sci-fi look with glowing neon colors.

## Result

A visually consistent promo reel that captures the energy of the event and carries the poster design into motion, made within three weeks. Music by Plattenlieferant, with sound tags by Plattenlieferant, S.3000 and the 3001 Nacht collective.

Tools: Blender · Human Generator · Mixamo · DaVinci Resolve
