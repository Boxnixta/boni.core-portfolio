<h2 className="not-prose project-section-title">Sneeze</h2>
<p className="not-prose project-section-meta">Loop animation · Character design · Blender & Grease Pencil · 2023</p>

Made in two weeks in a short project with Prof. Mathias Jüsche: an endlessly looping animation inspired by a speculative Y3K aesthetic. I developed my own character including 3D model, posing, rigging and frame-by-frame animation with Grease Pencil. Light, sound and camera movement turn it into a funny, atmospheric loop where motion, style and sound melt together.

<h2 className="not-prose project-section-title">Undine</h2>
<p className="not-prose project-section-meta">Digital fashion · Blender · 2021</p>

In this experimental project I explored the future of the fashion industry, inspired by haute couture and digital fashion. "Undine" is a purely digital garment with a glowing warm core and floating drop elements that create an almost timeless atmosphere. Virtual space opens new, sustainable perspectives on fashion, beyond wear and material consumption.
