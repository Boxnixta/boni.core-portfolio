The comic connects personal anecdotes with social observation and turns experiences of being looked at, crossed boundaries and self-assertion into small visual-poetic miniatures. The style is deliberately variable, raw and spontaneous. "Klein Sein" is not a complaint but an invitation to shift perspective, and a humorous form of empowerment through visibility.
