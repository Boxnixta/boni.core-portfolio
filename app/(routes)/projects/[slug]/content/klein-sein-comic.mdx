## Concept

The comic connects personal anecdotes with social observation and turns experiences of being looked at, crossed boundaries and self-assertion into small visual-poetic miniatures. The illustration style is deliberately variable, raw and spontaneous, a visual counterpart to the openness and vulnerability of the stories. "Klein Sein" is not a complaint but an invitation to shift perspective, and a humorous form of empowerment through visibility.
