## The Entity

The entity is an abstract, non-humanoid presence: a floating energy sphere of branching, lightning-like structures inside a translucent volume. It pulses and reacts visually as the player progresses.

## Environment & Guidance

A dream-like pink terrain built with Unity's terrain system and recolored textures, combined with force-field shaders that give trees and stones a white shimmer. Instead of UI or markers, pulsing green spheres guide the player along two paths, while areas off the paths invite free exploration. A generated voice calls for help and draws the player toward the center of the world.

## Puzzle

Around a central stone structure stand mirror-like columns, one of them broken. The player collects the scattered shards with ray-based grabbing and places them on the column, where they snap into place. With the last shard, the entity ascends and leaves the world through a newly appearing wind tunnel.
