## Process

The process covered 3D modeling, posing and rigging with weight painting, and detailed frame-by-frame animation with Grease Pencil. Lighting, camera moves and a matching soundtrack gave the film its atmosphere. The focus was on seamless loops and a harmonious interplay of motion, style and sound.

Tools: Blender · Grease Pencil
