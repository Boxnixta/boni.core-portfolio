## Technical Highlights

- **Procedural environment:** An inverted sphere with a generated canvas texture using digital noise and dithering
- **Glass:** MeshPhysicalMaterial with dispersion, IOR and frosted effects
- **Post-processing:** UnrealBloomPass lets the inner dots glow through the glass
- **Math:** The dots are aligned on the curved sphere surfaces using normal vectors and quaternions

## Learnings

My focus was on simulating realistic glass. I used AI to understand the mathematical concepts like quaternion alignment and to debug the build step by step, which taught me how to build a Three.js render stack modularly.
