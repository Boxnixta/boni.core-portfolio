## Technical Highlights

- **Procedural environment:** an inverted sphere with a generated canvas texture using noise and dithering
- **Glass:** MeshPhysicalMaterial with dispersion, IOR and frosted effects
- **Post-processing:** UnrealBloomPass lets the inner dots glow through the glass
- **Math:** the dots sit exactly on the curved spheres using normal vectors and quaternions
