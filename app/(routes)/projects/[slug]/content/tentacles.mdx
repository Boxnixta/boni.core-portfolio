## Sound

Made in Ableton Live at 120 BPM. An ambient opening with a pluck arp and granulated vocals panning through space. From 0:15 a hard bounce beat enters and withdraws again, creating tension and release. A custom Vital synth with slow LFO pitch modulation creates a rippling, water-like motion. Different frequency bands get their own LFO speeds and panning, mimicking the decentralized sensing of an octopus.

## Visuals

Made in Blender with Cycles and finished in DaVinci Resolve. Two tentacle systems: procedural Geometry Nodes spirals driven by an empty that tracks the beat, and hand-drawn curves with noise displacement for constant wobbling. At the midpoint the world shifts from black to soft rose, turning mystery into revelation.

## Learnings

Geometry Nodes are flexible but expensive to render, so adaptive sampling was essential. Blender has no native audio input, so drivers on empties became a non-destructive way to sync animation to sound. It was also my first time building a synth in Vital.
